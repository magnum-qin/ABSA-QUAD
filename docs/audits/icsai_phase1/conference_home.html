About ICSAI 2026

 The 2026 12th International Conference on Systems and Informatics (ICSAI 2026) will take place from 19-21 December 2026, in Taicang, China.
 Separated from Shanghai's Jiading District by only a river, Taicang is one of China's most dynamic port cities, renowned for its unique maritime-river culture and modern development. This city boasts numerous famous landmarks: such as the Tianfei Palace in Liuhe, which bears the legacy of Yuan Dynasty maritime transport; the Sanjia Zheng He Memorial Hall, which houses extensive historical records of Zheng He's voyages. Visitors can also explore the Taicang Port container terminal, the Shaxi Ancient Town, the Modern Agricultural Park, and the Taicang Rothenburg Street.

 Taicang is located on the eastern coast of China, along the south bank of the Yangtze River estuary, and is administered by Jiangsu Province. Taicang's port advantages complement its Jiangnan cultural heritage in perfect harmony. The city's rich maritime history, rapid port-driven economic development, and favorable conditions for international exchange make it an ideal venue for exploring fields such as economics, urban planning, international trade, and international relations. As the largest foreign trade port on the Yangtze River and a crucial river-sea intermodal transport hub in the Yangtze River Delta, Taicang welcomes scholars and guests from around the world with its open and inclusive spirit.
