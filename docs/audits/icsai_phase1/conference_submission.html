Submission

——————————————————————
　　All submissions will be peer-reviewed by 3 experts in the field based on originality, significance, quality and clarity. Authors should use the Latex style files or MS-Word templates obtained from the conference site to format their papers. Authors should submit pdf files of their manuscripts via the online submission system. Submissions imply that the papers have not been submitted elsewhere and will not be submitted elsewhere for publications before the review decisions of this conference.

　　 Online Submission
　　All papers must be written in English. Download the instructions and sample file.
　　There are no page limits for initial submissions. For accepted papers, one registration covers 6 pages and any additional pages will incur extra page charges.
