Final Best Setup

The final system is a per-dataset mix rather than one global version.

Main takeaways

  • V5 adds back-translation and dual-layer ensembling, raising the 8-dataset mean to 0.4052.
  • V6 transfer learning and Japanese MeCab post-processing raise the mean to 0.4397, mostly through jpn, rus, tat, and ukr.
  • V13 variants show that augmentation must be language-specific: paraphrase helps jpn/rus, BT plus paraphrase helps ukr, and V6 remains best for tat at that stage.
  • V15 XLM-R-large is strong for rus, tat, and jpn. V16 native encoders are the largest single gain for jpn and zho_laptop, but regress on rus and eng_laptop.
  • Five-seed ensembling gives a positive gain on all eight datasets, with a mean gain of +4.76 pp.

Best configuration summary

  • eng_restaurant: DeBERTa-v3-large plus 5-seed ensemble.
  • eng_laptop: V5/V6 DeBERTa-v3-base, back-translation, dual-layer 5-seed ensemble.
  • jpn_hotel: Japanese DeBERTa-v2-large, no augmentation, vote=4.
  • rus_restaurant: XLM-R-large with vote and top-k sweep.
  • tat_restaurant: XLM-R-large with vote and top-k sweep.
  • ukr_restaurant: V13b, BT plus paraphrase.
  • zho_restaurant: V16 Chinese RoBERTa 5-seed ensemble.
  • zho_laptop: Chinese RoBERTa large with topk=0.10.
