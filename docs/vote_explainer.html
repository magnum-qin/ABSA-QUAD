Why Different Languages Use Different Thresholds
Language Optimal Vote vote=3 cF1 Optimal cF1 Gain Reason
rus_restaurant 5 0.4542 0.4826 +2.84% Seeds highly consistent after Chinese→Russian transfer. Strict filtering removes noise.
ukr_restaurant 5 0.4575 0.4706 +1.31% Similar to Russian — close language family, high seed agreement.
tat_restaurant 4 0.4471 0.4630 +1.59% Turkic language, slightly less consistent than Slavic. vote=4 balances well.
jpn_hotel 2 + MeCab 0.2920 0.3312 +3.92% 44 categories, small dataset, domain mismatch. Seeds disagree often → low threshold preserves recall. MeCab fixes boundary errors.
eng / zho 3 — — — Default majority vote works well for high-resource languages with ample training data.
Key takeaway: Vote threshold tuning requires zero retraining — it is a pure inference-time change. Total gain across all languages: approximately +0.007 average cF1 at no additional computational cost.
